SECTION 1: HARDWARE COMPATIBILITY & SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS (Page 1)
Q: What operating systems are compatible with TechNova NovaBook and NovaHub?
A: TechNova devices are fully certified for Windows 11 (64-bit), macOS 13 Ventura and newer, Ubuntu LTS 22.04+, and ChromeOS. Android and iOS companion apps are available for wireless peripheral synchronization.

Q: Can the memory (RAM) and SSD storage be upgraded on the NovaBook 15 Pro?
A: The NovaBook 15 Pro features two user-accessible DDR5 SO-DIMM slots supporting up to 64GB RAM (5600MHz). Storage includes two M.2 NVMe PCIe 4.0 slots (one populated from factory, one vacant for expansion). Opening the bottom casing using the provided Torx T5 driver does not void the hardware warranty provided internal components are not physically damaged.

SECTION 2: BATTERY LIFE & CHARGING SPECIFICATIONS (Page 2)
Q: How long does the battery last on a single charge?
A: The NovaBook 15 Pro features an 84Wh battery providing up to 14 hours of continuous productivity workflows (web browsing and document editing at 200 nits display brightness), or approximately 8 hours of video playback.

Q: What charger should be used?
A: A 100W USB-C Power Delivery (PD 3.0) GaN charger is included. The device can fast charge from 0% to 50% in approximately 32 minutes. Third-party USB-C chargers with a minimum 65W PD rating can be used in low-power charging mode.

SECTION 3: CLOUD SYNC & SOFTWARE SUBSCRIPTIONS (Page 3)
Q: Is a paid subscription required to use TechNova devices?
A: No, basic hardware operation, driver updates, and offline firmware features do not require any recurring subscription.
Optional TechNova Cloud Plus ($4.99/month) provides 2TB encrypted cloud backup, remote wipe capabilities, and automated real-time configuration sync across unlimited devices.
