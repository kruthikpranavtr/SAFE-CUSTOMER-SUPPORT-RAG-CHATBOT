SECTION 2: ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT & PASSWORDS (Page 2)
Q: How do I reset my TechNova account password?
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login screen, enter your registered email address, and follow the one-time authentication link sent to your inbox. The link expires after 15 minutes for security.

Q: How can I update my shipping address after placing an order?
A: If your order was placed within the past 30 minutes, you can edit the address directly in your order confirmation portal. Beyond 30 minutes, please contact live support immediately so an agent can attempt manual address correction before carrier dispatch.
