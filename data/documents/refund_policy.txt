SECTION 1: OVERVIEW AND ELIGIBILITY (Page 1)
TechNova strives to ensure customer satisfaction with all hardware and software purchases. If you are not entirely satisfied with your purchase, our refund policy outlines the terms and procedures for requesting a return and refund.
To be eligible for a refund:
1. The request must be submitted within 7 calendar days of delivery.
2. The item must be in its original, undamaged packaging with all included accessories, manuals, and protective films.
3. Proof of purchase (such as your TechNova order confirmation number or invoice) is strictly required for all refund requests.
4. Software licenses and downloadable digital keys are eligible for refund only if the license key has not been activated or redeemed.

SECTION 2: PROCESSING TIMES AND RETURN SHIPPING (Page 2)
Once your returned item is received at our inspection facility, our quality control team will inspect the item within 3 business days.
If approved:
- Refunds will be credited to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days, depending on your card issuer or banking institution.
- Customers within the domestic United States receive complimentary prepaid return shipping labels for defective or wrong items. For change-of-mind returns, a flat $8.50 restocking and return shipping fee will be deducted from the total refund amount.
- Expedited shipping fees paid at checkout are strictly non-refundable.

SECTION 3: NON-REFUNDABLE ITEMS AND EXCEPTIONS (Page 3)
The following items are strictly non-refundable:
1. Gift cards and promotional promotional vouchers.
2. Custom configured desktop towers and personalized engraved accessories.
3. Items damaged due to customer misuse, liquid exposure, or unauthorized third-party repairs.
4. Products returned after the 7-day post-delivery eligibility window has expired.

SECTION 4: INTERNATIONAL RETURNS (Page 4)
TechNova currently does NOT support automated prepaid return shipping labels for international returns. International customers are responsible for all return shipping postage, customs duties, import tariffs, and local brokerage fees. International return requests must still be initiated within 7 calendar days of international delivery through support@technova-support-demo.com.
