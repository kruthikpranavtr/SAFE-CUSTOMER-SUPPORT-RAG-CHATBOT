SECTION 1: ORDER PROCESSING AND FULFILLMENT (Page 1)
All domestic and international orders are processed during standard business hours (Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST), excluding recognized federal holidays.
- Standard processing time: 1 to 2 business days before warehouse dispatch.
- Orders placed after 2:00 PM EST on Friday will be processed on the following Monday morning.
- Once processed, tracking numbers are automatically dispatched via email.

SECTION 2: SHIPPING METHODS AND DELIVERY TIMELINES (Page 2)
TechNova provides three primary shipping options for domestic delivery:
1. Standard Ground Shipping: Takes 3 to 5 business days for delivery after warehouse dispatch. Free on domestic orders exceeding $50.00; otherwise $5.99 flat rate.
2. Expedited Express Shipping: Takes 2 business days for guaranteed delivery after dispatch. Flat fee of $14.99.
3. Priority Overnight Shipping: Guaranteed next business day delivery if placed before 12:00 PM EST. Flat fee of $29.99.

SECTION 3: INTERNATIONAL SHIPPING (Page 3)
TechNova ships to over 45 supported countries across North America, Europe, Australia, and parts of Asia.
- International standard delivery: 7 to 14 business days depending on customs clearance.
- Customs fees, VAT, and import tariffs: Depending on the destination jurisdiction, import duties may be collected upon delivery by the postal carrier. TechNova does not cover destination customs charges unless DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) was specifically selected at checkout.

SECTION 4: DAMAGED OR LOST SHIPMENTS (Page 4)
If your package arrives physically damaged or is marked as delivered by the carrier but cannot be located:
- File a report with our support team within 48 hours of carrier delivery confirmation.
- Include photographs of the outer shipping box, inner foam packing, and damaged contents.
- TechNova will launch an immediate carrier trace and issue a priority replacement within 2 business days upon verification.
