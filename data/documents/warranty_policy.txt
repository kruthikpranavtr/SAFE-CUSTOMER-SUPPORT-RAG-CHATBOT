DOCUMENT: Warranty Policy
DOCUMENT ID: POL-WRT-2024-V2
LAST UPDATED: January 20, 2024

SECTION 1: STANDARD HARDWARE WARRANTY PERIOD (Page 1)
All brand-new TechNova hardware devices come with a standard 1-year (12-month) limited manufacturer warranty from the date of confirmed delivery.
Refurbished and certified pre-owned units carry a 90-day limited warranty covering core motherboard, display, and power components.

SECTION 2: WHAT IS COVERED (Page 2)
The standard warranty covers:
1. Factory defects in materials and manufacturing artistry.
2. Hardware component failures occurring during normal, intended operational use (e.g., dead pixels numbering 3 or more, faulty thermal fans, power supply failures).
3. Battery capacity degradation falling below 80% of original rated design capacity during the first 12 months.
TechNova will repair the defect or replace the unit with a new or factory-recertified equivalent unit free of charge.

SECTION 3: WHAT IS NOT COVERED (EXCLUSIONS) (Page 3)
The warranty strictly excludes:
1. Cosmetic wear and tear, superficial scratches, dents, or broken plastic ports from drop impact.
2. Damage caused by accidents, lightning strikes, power surges, or water and liquid spills.
3. Unapproved modifications or unauthorized firmware flashing outside TechNova official releases.
4. Loss or corruption of customer data stored on internal storage drives. Customers are strongly urged to maintain independent off-site backups prior to warranty servicing.

SECTION 4: EXTENDED WARRANTY (TECHNOVA CARE+) (Page 4)
Customers can purchase TechNova Care+ within 30 days of hardware purchase. TechNova Care+ extends warranty coverage to 3 full years and includes two incidents of accidental damage handling per year subject to a $49.00 deductible.
